# References and attribution

These links identify external providers and guidance relevant to the project. Linked content is not bundled, and listing a source does not claim permission to use or redistribute its datasets.

## Publication and data-use guidance

- [GitHub: Licensing a repository](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository) — public visibility and licensing are separate decisions.
- [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) — copyright permission, attribution, and rights outside the license.
- [AI Hub data-use policy](https://aihub.or.kr/intrcn/guid/usagepolicy.do?currMenu=151&topMenu=105) — provider-specific acquisition, use, sharing, and transfer conditions.

These guidance pages were reviewed for this portfolio on 17 October 2026. Prior source-specific audits remain historical records; they were not all refreshed for this documentation-only preparation.

## Selected research sources considered

- [PolyAI BANKING77](https://github.com/PolyAI-LDN/task-specific-datasets) and [Casanueva et al., Efficient Intent Detection with Dual Sentence Encoders](https://aclanthology.org/2020.nlp4convai-1.5/) — banking intent queries, not independently established fraud/normal call labels.
- [UCI SMS Spam Collection](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/228/sms+spam+collection) — spam/ham messages; spam is not automatically phishing.
- [Cho and Seo, Towards Reliable and Practical Phishing Detection](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-industry.18/) — relevant Korean phishing research; public paper access is distinct from dataset permission.
- [Korean Voice Phishing Detection / KorCCVi research repository](https://github.com/selfcontrol7/Korean_Voice_Phishing_Detection) — source recordings and derived data require separate rights review.
- [Weber et al., Anti-Money Laundering in Bitcoin](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02591) — Elliptic transaction-graph methodology, not domestic voice-phishing calls or accounts.

External authors, dataset creators, and library maintainers retain their respective contributions. The portfolio does not imply their participation, endorsement, or sponsorship.
