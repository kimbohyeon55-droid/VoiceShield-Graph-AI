# Project status and evidence boundaries

Portfolio review date: **17 October 2026**.

## Two tracks in the inspected workspace

**Real-data preparation:** Discovery, rights review, download evidence, canonicalization, and quality reports are present. The recorded readiness decision is STILL_BLOCKED for the intended Korean training task. Public English messages and intent queries are not evidence of an approved Korean voice-phishing corpus.

**Synthetic experimentation:** Later local scripts and stored summaries show conversation-model, temporal, graph, and explanation prototypes. Relevant summaries identify their scope as synthetic and not validated in the real world. They are acknowledged as research prototypes, without reproducing their metrics.

## Documentation drift

The top-level status document still describes the earlier harvest milestone and says synthetic generation and model work had not occurred in that step. Later files show that additional synthetic work exists. This is a chronology/documentation gap, not a reason to erase earlier records or to present later work as validated real-data research.

The central claim-evidence file is header-only. Stored metric JSON and freeze markers alone do not resolve the full experiment sequence, independence of evaluation, or publication rights. The portfolio therefore contains no numerical model-performance claims.

## What this portfolio demonstrates

- A research problem and a modular approach to conversation, timing, relationships, and explanations.
- Documented data engineering and explicit decisions to withhold ineligible data.
- Awareness of duplicate leakage, synthetic artifacts, attribution, and publication boundaries.
- An honest distinction between observed prototypes, recorded results, and validated public claims.

## What it does not establish

Production readiness, live-call operation, safe autonomous intervention, effective real-world fraud prevention, independently validated Korean detection performance, or unrestricted rights to any excluded source code or dataset.

Before stronger claims can be published, the project needs eligible real data, an appropriate independent evaluation, reconciled experiment records, and evidence tied to each claim. Before code can be published, its ownership and licensing must be established file by file.
